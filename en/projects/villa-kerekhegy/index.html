01New build · Family house

Villa Kerekhegy

Budakeszi · 2024

A two-storey family house on a steep north–south slope, with a panoramic living level and a garage buried into the hillside.

With an 18% slope, the house doesn't sit on the hillside — it sits in it. The garage and plant room went below grade, which put the living level exactly at the height of the view.

The structure combines a monolithic reinforced concrete raft with masonry walls above. The uninterrupted run of glazing along the south elevation required steel transfer beams, agreed with the structural engineer while the design was still a sketch.

Construction took 14 months and we handed over two weeks ahead of the contract date. The client sourced their own floor finishes and kitchen; we built that into the programme from the start.

What we did

  • Earthworks and retaining walls
  • Monolithic RC raft and basement level
  • Masonry structure with steel transfer beams
  • Flat roof with extensive green cover
  • Complete MEP and electrical installation
  • Interior finishes, painting, handover
