Courtyard House

Gödöllő · 2022

A U-shaped house organised around a sheltered courtyard on a plot beside a busy road, built for two generations.

The weakness of the plot was the noisy main road. The plan answered it: the house turns inward in a U, showing the street nothing but a closed elevation and service rooms.

On the courtyard side a continuous sliding glass wall runs the length of the living space, so in summer there is no hard line between inside and garden. The courtyard paving is permeable and rainwater soaks away on site.

The house holds two independent dwellings with separate entrances and kitchens but a shared courtyard — the older generation lives independently, still under one roof.

What we did

  • Acoustic build-up on the street elevation
  • Masonry structure with monolithic RC slabs
  • Large-format sliding glass walls
  • Two separately metered MEP systems
  • Permeable courtyard paving and landscaping
  • Turnkey handover of both dwellings
