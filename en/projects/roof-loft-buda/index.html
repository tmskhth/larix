05Loft conversion

Rózsadomb Loft

Budapest, District II · 2022

A loft conversion in an occupied apartment building, with craned material handling and strict quiet hours.

The hard part wasn't the structure — it was that people lived below us. We committed to craning materials in from outside and to working strictly between 8am and 4pm, with complete silence at weekends.

The existing timber floor couldn't carry the new loads, so we installed a steel beam grid that transfers them onto the main walls. The structural design was approved by the building's owners' association too.

Instead of roof windows we built two terraces recessed into the roof plane, giving the upper level open sky — an approach the district design review board supported.

What we did

  • Structural design and owners' association liaison
  • Steel load-bearing structure
  • Craned material handling, protection of the occupied building
  • Two recessed roof terraces
  • Complete MEP and electrical installation
  • Interior finishes and occupancy permit
