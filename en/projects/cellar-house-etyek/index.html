02Renovation & extension

The Cellar House

Etyek · 2023

A hundred-year-old wine press house restored and extended, keeping the original rammed walls and adding a glazed link to the new wing.

The original walls are a mix of adobe and stone; a structural engineer surveyed them before any demolition to decide what could stay. In the end the street elevation and the vaulted cellar were kept in full.

The extension deliberately doesn't imitate the old building: the new wing has a bare concrete plinth and charred timber cladding, joined to the original by a glazed link. The history of the house stays readable from the outside.

Rising damp was treated with retrofit wall injection and internal calcium silicate boards — we deliberately left out any vapour barrier so the structure can keep drying out.

What we did

  • Structural survey and reinforcement
  • Retrofit damp-proofing and moisture strategy
  • Restoration of the vaulted cellar
  • Timber-frame extension with charred cladding
  • Glazed connecting link
  • Full MEP and interior fit-out
