01What we do

Four services, one point of contact.

You don't have to line up a bricklayer, a carpenter and an electrician yourself. You sign one contract with us, and you call one person when you have a question.

01

General contracting

A turnkey family home from the foundation slab to the final clean. We coordinate every trade; you see one schedule and one invoice.

  • Fixed-price contractor agreement
  • Weekly photo progress report
  • Full coordination of subcontractors
02

Structural work

If you want to handle the finishes yourself, we build the house to weathertight shell — structurally sound and fully documented.

  • Strip and raft foundations
  • Masonry and reinforced concrete structures
  • Roof structure and covering
03

Renovation & extension

Modernising older houses without erasing their character. A structural engineer and an MEP consultant assess the building before anything is demolished.

  • Full interior renovation
  • Loft conversions and extensions
  • Structural reinforcement, retrofit damp-proofing
04

Design & permits

With our architect partners we take you from the first sketch through the Hungarian e-log permit process, paperwork included.

  • Network of architects and structural engineers
  • Permitting, e-log, authority liaison
  • Realistic cost estimate at concept stage

03How we work

Five steps to the keys.

No surprises halfway through. At the end of every stage you know what comes next, what it costs and when it will be done.

We visit the plot, look at what you're working with, and tell you honestly if something won't work. This step is free.

Our architect partner produces a concept design and we price it line by line. You don't get one round number, you get a spreadsheet.

A fixed-price contract with the week-by-week programme and the technical scope attached. What isn't in it now won't appear in it later.

A photo progress report every week and a site walk every month. You have one project manager, not a call centre.

A joint technical handover with a snag list, full documentation and the occupancy permit. The structure carries a ten-year warranty.

04About us

We started on the tools, so we know why builds slip.

Larix Építő was founded in 2009 by two people: a bricklayer and a civil engineer. For the first five years we were on the scaffold ourselves, which is exactly how we know that delays are almost never caused by the weather — they're caused by something not being ordered in time.

There are twenty-four of us now, with our own masonry and carpentry crews. We've worked with the same MEP and electrical subcontractors for ten years — we don't pick people by reverse auction.

We take on six to eight houses a year. That limit is deliberate: it's what our two project managers can visit every single week.

Fixed price, itemised budget

The contract comes with an open-book cost breakdown. Extra work only ever happens against a written order.

Weekly photo report

Every Friday you get twenty to thirty photos and a short written summary of what was completed.

Our own crews

The structural work is done by our own team. We don't pass the job on to a crew you've never met.

Six to eight houses a year

We don't grow faster than we can keep the quality up. That's why we sometimes say no.

07Frequently asked

Worth knowing before you request a quote.

Turnkey delivery typically runs 850–1,100 thousand HUF per m², depending on specification. From the concept design we produce an itemised open-book budget — a table you can audit line by line, not one round number.

We take on six to eight houses a year and usually book 3–5 months ahead. The exact start date goes into the contract — together with a delay penalty.

Beyond the statutory warranty we give ten years on the structure. At handover you receive the full documentation: site log, measurements, declarations of conformity.
