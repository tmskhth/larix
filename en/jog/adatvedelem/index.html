Privacy Policy

Effective: 16 August 2026 · version 1.0

This notice explains, in plain language, what personal data Larix Építő Kft. (the “Controller”) processes when operating larix.hu and receiving quote requests, for what purposes, on what legal bases and for how long. It is based on Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and Hungarian Act CXII of 2011 (Infotv.).

The Hungarian version of this notice is authoritative; this English text is a courtesy translation.

2. The quote-request form

Data provided on the form: name, e-mail address, phone number (optional), project type and your message. Purpose: responding to your enquiry, preparing a quote and contacting you. Legal basis: GDPR Article 6(1)(b) — steps taken at your request prior to entering into a contract.

Providing your name and contact details is a precondition of preparing a quote; without them we cannot respond. Please include only information necessary for the quote in the free-text message.

3. Photo upload

You may optionally upload photos of the site. The legal basis is your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)), which you may withdraw at any time by writing to hello@larix.hu; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of prior processing.

Photographs may qualify as personal data where a person or an identifying detail (house number, licence plate) is recognisable. Please do not upload images showing identifiable third parties. We do not use image metadata (EXIF, including any GPS coordinates) and delete it during processing.

4. Retention

  • Quote requests that do not lead to a contract: deleted within 90 days after the 30-day validity of the quote expires.
  • Where a contract is concluded: contract-related data is kept for the general limitation period of 5 years (Civil Code § 6:22).
  • Data underlying accounting records (contract, invoice): 8 years under § 169 of Act C of 2000 on Accounting; legal basis GDPR Article 6(1)(c).

5. Recipients and processors

We do not sell personal data and do not share it with third parties for marketing. Our processors:

  • GitHub, Inc. (88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA) — website hosting (GitHub Pages).
  • Google Ireland Ltd. (Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) — e-mail service receiving form submissions.

6. Transfers to third countries

The hosting provider, GitHub, Inc., operates in the United States. Safeguards: GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (Commission adequacy decision (EU) 2023/1795), and GitHub's Data Protection Agreement additionally incorporates the Standard Contractual Clauses under GDPR Article 46(2)(c). Details: github.com/site/privacy and dataprivacyframework.gov; copies of the safeguards are available on request.

7. Host logging (IP addresses)

Although the site uses no cookies or analytics, GitHub logs the IP address of every visitor for security purposes, regardless of whether the visitor is signed in to GitHub. IP addresses are personal data. For these logs GitHub, Inc. acts as controller; see GitHub's Privacy Statement.

8. Cookies and localStorage

The site uses no cookies, analytics or tracking, so no cookie banner is required. A single localStorage entry stores your display-version preference; it contains no personal data, never leaves your browser, and is strictly necessary for a feature you request, so no consent is required under § 155(4) of Act C of 2003.

9. Your rights

  • Access (Article 15), rectification (16), erasure (17), restriction (18), data portability (20 — applicable to processing based on Article 6(1)(b)), objection (21), and withdrawal of consent (Article 7(3)) where consent is the basis (photo upload).
  • Submit requests to hello@larix.hu. We respond without undue delay and within one month at the latest (extendable by two months for complex requests). Exercising your rights is free of charge except for manifestly unfounded or excessive requests.

10. Automated decision-making

We use no automated decision-making or profiling (GDPR Article 22).

11. Data security

The site is served over HTTPS. Incoming enquiries are accessible only to staff involved in preparing quotes, under access controls (GDPR Article 32).

12. Remedies

You may lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority:

  • Nemzeti Adatvédelmi és Információszabadság Hatóság (NAIH)
  • Address: Falk Miksa utca 9–11., 1055 Budapest, Hungary
  • Postal: 1363 Budapest, Pf. 9. · Phone: +36 1 391 1400
  • E-mail: ugyfelszolgalat@naih.hu · Web: www.naih.hu
  • You may also bring the matter before the regional court of your place of residence (Infotv. § 23, GDPR Article 79).

13. Final provisions

The Controller may amend this notice unilaterally; the current version is always available on the website with its effective date. The GDPR and the Infotv. govern this notice; the Hungarian version prevails.
